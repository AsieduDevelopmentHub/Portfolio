Novend POS

Building business software around local operations, inventory, and synchronization across devices.

Role
Creator and developer
Status
Active product development

By Asiedu Minta Kwaku

The problem

Retail teams need to keep working when connectivity is unreliable. Sales, product lookup, and inventory workflows need a local foundation, with cloud synchronization supporting the business when a connection is available.

My role

I develop Novend POS across its application architecture and user-facing workflows. My work includes local data storage, synchronization, inventory, multi-branch operations, and integration with point-of-sale hardware.

The engineering approach

The architecture brings together a Next.js application, IndexedDB through Dexie for local storage, and Convex for cloud data. Electron supports desktop delivery, while Capacitor supports the mobile work. Local operations and an outgoing operation queue are central to the synchronization design.

Decisions that matter

Offline software needs to handle more than cached screens. Reconnection, conflicting stock changes, organization boundaries, and hardware differences all affect whether a cashier can finish a task. These are ongoing engineering concerns in the product, rather than problems solved by simply adding a service worker.

What this demonstrates

This project brings together business workflows, distributed data, and device integration. It is the strongest example of my interest in software that remains useful under real operating constraints.
