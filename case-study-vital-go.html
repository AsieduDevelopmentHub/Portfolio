The problem

A connected-health platform needs to turn data from devices into information people can understand. That requires coordinated work across device integration, data models, backend services, and patient and clinician experiences.

My role

As Co-founder and CTO, I guide Vital-Go’s technology direction across software and connected devices. My focus is how those parts fit together, from incoming health data to the interfaces used to review it.

The engineering approach

The platform is being developed around wearable data, device management, dashboards, and alerts. Integration evaluation considers user authorization, supported data types, synchronization, and how different device sources fit into a common model.

Current scope

Vital-Go is in active development. Third-party wearable integration is an evaluation and development focus, alongside the longer-term device work. Vendor support and production access depend on the relevant provider agreements and capabilities; no partnership approval is implied here.

What this demonstrates

The work connects hands-on engineering with technical leadership: defining interfaces, coordinating software and hardware decisions, and keeping a product architecture aligned with its intended users. Visit the Vital-Go website for public product information.
